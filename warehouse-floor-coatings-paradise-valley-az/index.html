What you get

Systems built for traffic.

Warehouse floors need build and hardness. We typically use a thick epoxy or polyurea body coat with a hard clear on top, choosing products for the equipment and chemicals in the space.

Prep includes grinding, joint repair and filling spalls that catch wheels. Markings for lanes, racking and keep-clear zones are laid out before the final clear so they wear well.

Typical build for this system, top to bottom. Select a layer.

Why choose it

What a coated warehouse floor does.

Less dust

Bare concrete powders over time, and a sealed floor keeps that off stock and machinery.

Clear lanes

Durable markings keep traffic organized and safer.

Easier cleaning

A sealed floor sweeps and scrubs much faster.

Better light

Light colors reflect light and brighten large spaces.

Options

Common warehouse builds.

We'll talk through both at the visit and bring samples so you can see them in your own light.

High-build epoxy

A thick, rigid system for storage and light industrial use. It's a cost-effective choice for large areas.

Polyurea system

A more flexible, impact-tolerant build for areas with heavy equipment, dropped loads or temperature changes.

Install day

How warehouse jobs are scheduled.

We agree on phases with your operations team, clear and protect each area, and grind with dust extraction. Joints and spalls are repaired, coats go down, and markings are laid out before the final clear.

Turnaround depends on size, system and phasing. Fast-cure options can return areas to service sooner.

Pricing

What affects warehouse pricing.

Large floors are priced on prep, system and logistics. These are the main factors.

  • Square footageLarger areas usually cost less per square foot.
  • Joint and spall repairDamaged joints need more repair work.
  • System buildThicker builds and polyurea add material.
  • Markings and phasingLines, zones and phased schedules add time.

Afterward

Looking after a coated warehouse floor.

Sweep or scrub regularly, keep forklift and pallet jack wheels clean, and repair damage early. Recoating the clear in high-traffic lanes extends the life of the system.
